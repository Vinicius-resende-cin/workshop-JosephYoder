Palestras:

  • Designing Agentic Systems: A Domain-Driven Approach to Language-Centric AI (com Kyle Brown)

    Resumo: Large Language Models (LLMs) have made natural language a primary interface for enterprise software, yet most agentic implementations remain ad hoc, brittle, and prone to "megaprompt" bloat—a modern recurrence of the classic Smart UI anti-pattern. To move from fragile experimentation to resilient architecture, we must treat language not just as an interface, but as core system infrastructure.

    This talk will demonstrate how to anchor agentic design in proven software engineering practices. You will learn how to map Domain-Driven Design (DDD) constructs—specifically Bounded Contexts and Ubiquitous Language—directly into Bounded Agents and Enterprise MCP Tools that safely wrap legacy APIs. We also recast agents as Model-View-Controller (MVC) controllers and introduce ANCHORS, seven foundational design principles (analogous to SOLID and IDEALS) that govern context ownership, collaborative agent networks, standardized protocols, single-team ownership, and mandatory Human-in-the-Loop safeguards. Leave with a repeatable blueprint for building scalable, governable hybrid AI systems that contain blast radius and prevent agent drift.

  • Cloud Application Architecture Patterns: Designing, Building, and Modernizing for the Cloud

    Resumo: There are more applications running in the cloud than there are ones that run well there. If you're considering taking advantage of cloud technology for your projects, this practical guide is an ideal way to understand the best practices that will help you architect applications that work well in the cloud, no matter which vendors, products, or languages you use. Architects and lead developers will learn how cloud applications should be designed, how they fit into a larger architectural picture, and how to make them operate efficiently.

  • Domain Modeling in the Age of AI
    Design and Model Deeply. Let AI Accelerate—Not Derail!

    Resumo: Generative AI produces code and artifacts at astonishing speeds—but speed without intentional boundaries yields synthetic technical debt and a distributed Big Ball of Mud. Domain first. AI second. Architecture always!

    In this hands-on mini-workshop, we distill the essential domain-modeling techniques for the AI era: defining clear Bounded Contexts, crafting a Ubiquitous Language that aligns humans and LLMs, running AI-assisted Event Storming, and using microscopic slicing as architectural guardrails. Leave with practical patterns to make AI a disciplined co-design partner rather than an agent of chaos.

Kyle Brown

Biografia: Kyle Brown is an IBM Fellow and Vice President and the CTO to the CIO of IBM, where he is responsible for Enterprise Architecture, Platform Strategy and Technical Vitality for the IBM CIO. He has been leading in the development and implementation of large-scale systems for over thirty years. He is a world-recognized expert on topics in cloud, Java, and software architecture. He publishes often, records podcasts, and is the author of eleven books including the newly released “Cloud Application Architecture Patterns” from O’Reilly.
